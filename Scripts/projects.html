The Challenge: As part of a strategic initiative with InsightSpark, the data engineering team required a preliminary analysis of three years' worth of supermarket transaction data. The goal was to validate specific purchasing trends before deploying the data into a larger engineering pipeline.

The Solution: I conducted a granular analysis using Excel to extract key financial insights. HR ANALYTICS & DATA TRANSFORMATION

Deloitte Compensation Equality Analysis

The Challenge: The client required an assessment of employee compensation across various factories and job roles. While raw "Equality Scores" existed, the data was difficult to interpret at a glance, making it hard to identify urgent cases of pay discrimination.

The Solution: I engineered a classification model within Excel to transform numerical scores into categorical insights. By applying nested conditional logic, I segmented the data into "Fair," "Unfair," and "Highly Discriminative" classes. This allowed stakeholders to instantly isolate high-risk areas and prioritize remediation efforts.
